# VXP-3 Brand System

## Name
- Product: Pedestrian Pursuit
- Internal art direction: KINETIC SOLE

## Concept
Footwear is the machine. The runner is the vehicle. Energetic, athletic, tactile, fast, playful, competitive, readable, stylish, optimistic, original, accessible.

- Grip: tread bars
- Speed: forward chevron
- Bounce: arc + ball
