6. Tutorial race uses contextual coaching; Pause keeps Tutorial Guide; completion contracts preserved.
7. Pause Resume is dominant; pause input semantics unchanged (no double-toggle).
8. Results podium uses VXP glyphs, not emoji medals.
9. Accessibility: preserve reduce-motion, larger UI, colorblind markers + shape redundancy.
10. Do not claim final art, esports balance, human playtest PASS, or rights clearance beyond authored VXP assets.
