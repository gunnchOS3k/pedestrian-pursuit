# Design Pillars — Pedestrian Pursuit

## 1. Feet Are the Vehicle

Players do not ride anything. Their feet handle acceleration, drifting, boosting, combat, stunts, terrain reactions, wall movement, rail grinding, recovery, and personality.

## 2. Easy to Learn, Hard to Master

Beginners enjoy the first race within seconds. Advanced players master Perfect Steps, Foot Drifts, boost chaining, shortcut routing, item defense, trick combos, terrain selection, and character-specific advantages.

## 3. Chaotic but Fair

Action-packed and silly, but not frustratingly random. Comeback tools exist for trailing racers; skilled players are still rewarded for clean racing.

## 4. Movement First

Movement feel comes before content quantity. Running, drifting, boosting, jumping, and landing must feel satisfying before adding more tracks or characters.

## 5. Tracks Are Playgrounds

Tracks are obstacle courses, playgrounds, sports arenas, rooftops, and extreme sports parks — not flat car roads with runners placed on top.

## Originality Rule

Do not copy characters, items, music, tracks, names, UI, models, or exact mechanics from existing franchises. Build original identity around foot-powered racing, shoe customization, and exaggerated running movement.
